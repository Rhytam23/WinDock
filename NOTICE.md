## What that means in practice

- **Sharing official WinDock** as downloaded from this repository's releases, unmodified, is fine, logo included.
- **Writing about WinDock** (a review, an article, a video) can use the name, the logo and screenshots to refer to it.
- **Forks and modified versions** are welcome under MIT, but give them their own name and icon, and don't present them as
  official WinDock. Replace the logo files and change the app icon drawing (the app-icon method in
  `src/Glyphs.cs`) before you distribute a build.
- **Reusing the videos, images or website design** in your own project needs permission. Ask via
  [GitHub issues](https://github.com/Rhytam23/WinDock/issues).
